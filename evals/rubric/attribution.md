Pass only if who said a claim appears as a `+3` child of that claim, and each such child restates the claim in full so it stands alone, such as "Joe said the sky looks blue". Fail if an attribution child refers back with wording like "this claim" or "this argument", or gives only a name, or if the attribution is missing from a claim about the world that the source attributes. A statement that is itself about the speaker's own reasoning process, such as which evidence a judge weighted most, already names its speaker and needs no attribution child.
